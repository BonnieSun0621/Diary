1. Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until  we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision  tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each  question, provide your recommended answer.
2. Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on  each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is  bewildering.
3. If a fact can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The decisions, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.
4. Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
